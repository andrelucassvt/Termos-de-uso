# Privacy Policy — Arrow Army

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Army ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it on iOS, Android, or other supported platforms. Arrow Army is a casual offline game, and we designed it to collect as little information as possible.

By downloading and playing Arrow Army, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Store on Your Device

Arrow Army stores the following information **locally on your device only**:

- Game progress (current level, coins, and purchased upgrades)
- Settings (sound and haptics preferences)

This information is saved using your device's standard local storage and is **never transmitted to us**. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot access it. It remains on your device until you delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game.

Arrow Army does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information directly.

## 2. Information Collected by Advertising Partners

Arrow Army shows advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. When ads are requested, Google and its partners may automatically collect information such as:

- Device information (model, operating system, language, and similar technical data)
- IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location)
- Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers when permitted)
- Information about your interactions with ads (views and clicks)
- Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

This information is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive, store, or control this information ourselves; Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

An internet connection is required only to load ads. The Game itself plays offline.

## 3. Consent and Your Choices

We use Google's consent management tools to respect the privacy rules that apply where you live.

- **EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** when required, the Game shows a consent form before requesting ads. You can review or change your choices at any time through the **Privacy** option in the in-game menu (shown when your region requires it).
- **iOS:** the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not made available to the ad SDK. You can change this permission anytime in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- You can also limit ad-related data collection by uninstalling the Game or by using your device's privacy controls.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information collected by our advertising partner is used to:

- Display ads within the Game (including limiting how often the same ad is shown)
- Measure ad performance
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and technical issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We use the locally stored information described in Section 1 solely to save your progress and settings on your device.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with third parties other than the advertising provider described in Section 2 (Google AdMob), which processes ad-related data as an independent service provider under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the public.

## 6. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal information on our own systems. Locally stored game data remains on your device until you clear it. Data collected by Google through ad serving is retained according to Google's privacy policy, which is linked in Section 2.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Arrow Army is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at anl.software.contato@gmail.com so we can address it.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the Game and its data practices safe. Because the information we store remains on your device and is not transmitted to us, its security depends primarily on your device's own protections, such as your passcode and operating system updates.

## 9. International Processing

Our advertising partner is a global company and may process ad-related data in countries other than yours, subject to the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
